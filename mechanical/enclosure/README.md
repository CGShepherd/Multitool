# Enclosure and Mechanical Design

Preferred approach is to adapt a COTS Raspberry Pi 5 or industrial HAT enclosure if practical.

Mechanical requirements:

- rigid metal construction;
- chassis-mounted 24-pin IEEE-488 connector;
- no significant GPIB cable load into Pi/HAT PCB;
- access to Raspberry Pi power, Ethernet and USB;
- access to programming/service interface;
- provision for passive or active cooling;
- sufficient HAT height and wiring volume;
- mechanically sound connector-shell bonding to chassis.

A bespoke enclosure is a fallback rather than the default.
