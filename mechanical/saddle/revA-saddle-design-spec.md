# Rev A U-Saddle Design Specification

## Provisional geometry

Final dimensions depend on Pi-Box CAD.

Starting envelope:
- internal clear width: Pi-Box width + 1 to 2 mm side clearance;
- saddle bridge width: approximately 112–116 mm;
- saddle depth: approximately 80–100 mm;
- saddle height above Pi-Box top: 30–45 mm;
- material: 2.0 mm aluminium preferred.

## Mounting
- Four HAT standoffs on standard pattern.
- Saddle fixes into structural enclosure features, never solely the removable lid.
- HAT mounting holes electrically isolated from CHASSIS unless deliberately bonded.

## Rear panel
- IEEE-488 connector cut-out and mounting holes.
- optional service connector positions.
- strain relief for internal GPIB loom.

## Ribbon entry
- protected slot sized for selected 40-way ribbon;
- radiused edges / grommet or edge trim;
- cable clamp so the Pi header carries no tensile load.

## Access
Top cover arrangement should permit DIP replacement, EEPROM jumper access, probing, ribbon removal and GPIB loom removal.
