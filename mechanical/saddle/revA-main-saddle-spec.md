# Rev A Main Saddle — Fabrication Specification

## Material
- 2.0 mm aluminium sheet.
- Prefer soft/formable alloy for home bending.

## Geometry
- Inverted U-section.
- Two 90-degree bends only.
- Approximate bridge width: Pi-Box external width plus side clearance.
- Two side cheeks extend down to upper front/rear enclosure fastener positions.

## Features
- four HAT mounting holes on standard HAT pattern;
- four Pi-Box attachment holes aligned with existing upper front/rear case fixings;
- four M3 connector-bracket attachment holes;
- ribbon-cable passage / strain-relief feature;
- optional top-cover mounting holes.

## Bend guidance
- inside radius target: 1.5–2.0 mm minimum;
- bend direction: both side cheeks downward;
- no compound bends.

## Finish
Black finish optional.
Maintain deliberate bare-metal/chassis bonding points if painted/anodised.
