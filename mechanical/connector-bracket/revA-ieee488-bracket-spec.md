# Rev A IEEE-488 Connector Bracket — Fabrication Specification

## Material
- 2.0 mm aluminium.

## Preferred construction
Flat plate first choice.
Add one 90-degree stiffening flange only if load testing shows excessive flex.

## Interfaces
- four M3 saddle attachment holes;
- IEEE-488 receptacle rectangular cut-out;
- IEEE-488 connector mounting holes;
- optional future service connector blank area.

## Structural requirement
A connected GPIB cable must not visibly flex the HAT PCB or transmit meaningful load to the HAT loom/header.

## Grounding
Connector shell -> bracket -> saddle -> CHASSIS.
Provide intentional metal-to-metal bond at one bracket fixing or dedicated bonding strap.

## Serviceability
Bracket must be removable with HAT still installed.
26-way loom must disconnect without removing the saddle.
