# Stage 2 Decision Register Additions

Merge these rows into `docs/decision-register.md`.

| ID | Decision | Status | Rationale |
|---|---|---|---|
| DR-027 | Use two TXS0108E devices as the provisional Pi 5 ↔ SN75160B/SN75161B level-translation architecture. | CLOSED (prototype validation required) | Handles independent bidirectional channels without duplicating SN75161 direction logic. |
| DR-028 | Translator A shall carry DIO1…DIO8; Translator B shall carry EOI, DAV, NRFD, NDAC, IFC, SRQ, ATN and REN. | CLOSED | Clean one-to-one mapping of the sixteen GPIB signals. |
| DR-029 | TXS0108E VCCA shall be 3.3 V and VCCB shall be 5 V. | CLOSED | Within TI specified supply ranges for 3.3↔5 V translation. |
| DR-030 | Both translator OE pins shall default LOW in hardware. | CLOSED | Keeps Pi isolated from SN7516x during boot/reset. |
| DR-031 | Do not require a custom gpib_bitbang GPIO solely to enable the translators. | CLOSED | Preserve upstream driver compatibility. |
| DR-032 | Translator enable should be generated by hardware power-good/delay unless later software integration offers a cleaner upstream-compatible mechanism. | OPEN IMPLEMENTATION | Avoids consuming/reassigning gpib4pi GPIOs. |
| DR-033 | TE, DC and PE do not require voltage up-translation; 3.3 V satisfies SN7516x TTL-high input requirements. | CLOSED | Datasheet threshold compatibility. |
| DR-034 | TXS0108E TSSOP-20 is acceptable for hand assembly provided a hand-solder-friendly footprint is used. | CLOSED | Two ICs greatly simplify the design compared with multi-package direction logic. |
