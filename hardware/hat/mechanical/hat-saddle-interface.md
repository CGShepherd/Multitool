# HAT-to-Saddle Interface

- Standard HAT-class board geometry and four mounting holes.
- Four removable M2.5 standoffs attached to saddle bridge.
- Target 8-10 mm clearance beneath HAT, subject to PCB review.
- HAT component side upward.
- 40-way ribbon connector toward cable entry.
- 26-way GPIB loom connector toward rear saddle panel where practical.
- Mounting hardware does not intentionally bond PCB GND to CHASSIS.
- No heavy panel connector mounted directly on HAT.
