# Sheet 08 — Test and Service Points

Provide accessible labelled points for:

Power:
- +5V_GPIB
- +3V3_PI
- GND
- CHASSIS

Control:
- GPIB_XLAT_EN
- GPIB_TE
- GPIB_DC
- GPIB_PE

Bus:
- DIO1
- DIO8
- DAV
- NRFD
- NDAC
- ATN
- IFC
- SRQ
- REN
- EOI

EEPROM:
- ID_SD
- ID_SC
- EEPROM_WP

Test points should be physically reachable with the Pi/HAT assembled where practical.
Power and ground points should accept normal oscilloscope probe hooks/minigrabbers.
